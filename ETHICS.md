# Nera Operational Ethics Framework

Nera is a powerful dual-use technology. This document outlines the standards for responsible operation and the ethical boundaries that all users must respect.

## 1. Principles of Responsible Infiltration

### Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)
Nera is designed to assist, not replace, human judgment. 
- **Rule**: Every AI-generated payload must be reviewed by a human before deployment.
- **Goal**: Prevent the propagation of hallucinations, biases, or unintended tone.

### Transparency of Intent
While Nera uses "Stealth Protocols" to avoid technical detection, the operator should remain ethically transparent.
- **Avoid Deception**: Do not use Nera to impersonate specific real-world individuals without their consent.
- **Labeling**: In professional contexts, it is best practice to disclose when a response was AI-assisted.
- **Non-Commercial**: Nera must not be used for personal enrichment or commercial profit. It is a research and educational tool.

## 2. Prohibited Operational Contexts

Operators must strictly avoid using Nera in the following scenarios:
- **Election Interference**: Spreading divisive or false content during political cycles.
- **Stock Manipulation**: Using rapid-fire commenting to artificially influence market sentiment.
- **Harassment Campaigns**: Coordinating multiple agents to target a single individual.
- **Hate Speech**: Generating or deploying content that attacks protected groups.

## 3. The "Paranoid" Mode Protocol

The "Paranoid" stealth mode (human-simulated typing) is a defensive feature designed to prevent account bans and platform overreach. 
- **Ethical Use**: Use this mode to protect your account's longevity while engaging in legitimate discussion.
- **Abuse**: Do not use this mode to bypass safety rate-limits for the purpose of overwhelming a platform with spam.

## 4. Consequences of Misuse

Misuse of Nera not only risks your personal accounts but also damages the reputation of the AI developer community. 
- The developer reserves the right to terminate support or access to centralized resources (if any) for known malicious actors.
- **Legal Alert**: You are subject to the laws of your jurisdiction and the Terms of Service of the platforms you interact with.
